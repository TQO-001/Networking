## Purpose and prerequisites

This is a specialisation track, not a prerequisite for the CCNA. Work through it after the relevant networking foundations are secure. Its purpose is to connect general networking knowledge to industrial environments and to develop safe documentation and visibility skills.

Use approved lab environments, fictional/sample records, or explicitly authorised read-only information. Follow workplace procedures, site rules, and change-control requirements. Do not actively probe, scan, reconfigure, or otherwise interact with production OT systems as a learning experiment.

## OT networking learning sessions

**This module is a specialisation, not a prerequisite for the CCNA.** Complete it after the relevant networking foundations are secure. Use only approved lab environments, sample data, or explicitly authorised read-only access.

**Outcome:** Apply core networking knowledge to industrial environments while respecting availability, safety, and change-control requirements.

- [ ] **OT-01 — IT and OT networking differences**  
  Compare typical priorities, lifecycle, availability, latency, change windows, asset constraints, and operational responsibilities. Understand why an IT-style change can have different consequences in an industrial environment.

- [ ] **OT-02 — Zones, conduits, and segmentation**  
  Learn the purpose of segmentation, security zones, conduits, industrial DMZ concepts, and controlled communication paths. Relate these ideas to VLANs, routing, ACLs, and firewalls.

- [ ] **OT-03 — Industrial network architecture**  
  Understand common industrial network components and the role of managed switches, redundancy, fibre, remote access gateways, and monitoring systems. Read architecture diagrams and identify trust boundaries.

- [ ] **OT-04 — Industrial protocols at a conceptual level**  
  Learn the basic purpose and architecture of protocols such as Modbus TCP and OPC UA. Focus on their roles, communication patterns, security considerations, and how they appear in network documentation.

- [ ] **OT-05 — Read-only OT inventory and visibility**  
  Design a safe inventory/reporting workflow using approved records or sample data. Capture device identity, IP, VLAN, switch/interface, protocol notes, and source of information. Do not actively probe or modify production control systems.

### OT integration project

- [ ] **OT-INT-01 — Industrial network documentation assistant**  
  Create a read-only tool that validates an approved CSV/JSON asset inventory, identifies missing or inconsistent fields, and generates a human-readable report. Include a clear record of where data came from and when it was collected.

## OT learning completion standard

You should be able to explain the purpose of the concept, identify it on a supplied architecture diagram, describe relevant trust boundaries and operational constraints, and produce documentation using sample or approved data. For the project, prioritise correctness, data provenance, and read-only behaviour over automation complexity.

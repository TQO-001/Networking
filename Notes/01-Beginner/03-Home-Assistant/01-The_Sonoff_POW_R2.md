The Sonoff POW R2 is a ==16A (15A rated in early specs) Wi-Fi smart switch with built-in power consumption monitoring==. It tracks real-time voltage, current, and power, logs historical energy usage data via the eWeLink app, and supports overload protection thresholds up to 3500W
![[Pasted image 20260826135110.jpg|309]]

## Key Specifications
- **Voltage Range:** 90–250V AC
- **Max Current:** 16A (15A/16A depending on region/label)
- **Max Power:** 3500W
- **Wireless Standard:** 802.11 b/g/n (2.4 GHz)
- **Safety Material:** ABS V-0 flame-retardant enclosure

## Core Features
- **Energy Metering:** Measures real-time power, current, and voltage, and calculates cumulative power usage.
- **Overload Protection:** Automatically turns off connected appliances if voltage, current, or power limits are crossed.
- **Data Export:** Stores up to 100 days of daily/hourly power records that can be exported to your phone.
- **Smart Control:** Compatible with the eWeLink app, Amazon Alexa, and Google Assistant.
- **Alternative Firmware:** Frequently flashed by users with custom open-source firmware like Tasmota or ESPHome (Note: exercise extreme caution with internal GPIO pins as they connect directly to live AC mains).

## Important interpretation notes

- A single large connected discovery component is not the same thing as a single department. It can span multiple physical areas and operational teams.
- The 11 single-switch groups had no resolved switch neighbour in this collection. That does not prove they are physically isolated; discovery may be incomplete or disabled.
- Keep unknown department/location labels provisional until confirmed onsite or by the responsible engineer.
- Use Department 34 as the documentation model, but do not copy its department label onto other switches without evidence.
- Preserve conflicting source values in a discrepancy log and resolve them with fresh approved records rather than guessing.

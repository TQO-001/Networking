# Definitions Drill Sheet — Batch 08: WAN & Wireless

---

### 1. LAN vs WAN
A LAN (Local Area Network) connects devices within a limited, typically single-site area (an office, a building) and is usually owned/managed entirely by one organization. A WAN (Wide Area Network) connects LANs together across larger geographic distances, typically relying on a service provider's infrastructure to carry traffic between sites.

---

### 2. 802.11 (Wi-Fi Standards)
802.11 is the family of IEEE standards that define how wireless local area networking works, with various amendments (802.11n, 802.11ac, 802.11ax/Wi-Fi 6, etc.) improving speed, range, and efficiency over time.

---

### 3. SSID (Service Set Identifier)
An SSID is the human-readable name broadcast by a wireless access point to identify a particular wireless network, which is what a device sees when scanning for available Wi-Fi networks to connect to.

---

### 4. WPA2 / WPA3
WPA2 and WPA3 are wireless security protocols that encrypt traffic between a device and an access point and control access to the wireless network via authentication. WPA3 is the newer standard, improving on WPA2's encryption strength and protection against certain attack types (like offline password guessing).

---

### 5. Wireless Access Point (AP)
A wireless access point is a device that allows wireless clients to connect to a wired network, effectively bridging the wireless and wired portions of the network. It can operate as a standalone (autonomous) AP or be managed centrally by a wireless controller.

---

### 6. Wireless LAN Controller (WLC)
A Wireless LAN Controller is a centralized device that manages multiple access points across a network — pushing configuration, managing radio settings, and coordinating roaming — instead of configuring each AP individually.

---

### 7. Roaming
Roaming is the process where a wireless client moves between coverage areas of different access points on the same wireless network without dropping its connection, ideally with minimal to no interruption.
